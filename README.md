# Simple Interest Calculator

## Introduction

The Simple Interest Calculator is a basic program that calculates the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

**Simple Interest (SI):**

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

* **P** = Principal Amount
* **R** = Rate of Interest per year
* **T** = Time period in years

**Total Amount:**

Amount = P + SI

## Example

Suppose:

* Principal = ₹10,000
* Rate = 5% per year
* Time = 2 years

Then:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

SI = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000

**Total Amount = ₹11,000**

## Features

* Easy to use
* Calculates simple interest quickly
* Calculates the total amount
* Suitable for beginners learning programming
* Uses the standard simple interest formula

## How It Works

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual rate of interest.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. The calculator applies the simple interest formula.
5. The simple interest and total amount are displayed.
